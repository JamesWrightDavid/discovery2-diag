# esp32/

Arduino firmware: a second K-line comms node and a passive sniffer.

## Files

- `README.md` — sketches and wiring (L9637D transceiver, UART2 on GPIO16/17).
- `kline_test/` — bring-up. `kline_sniff/` — RX-only tap used for reference-tool capture.
- `kline_node/` — full node. `signals_td5.h` is generated by `tools/gen_signal_header.py`.

## Editing rules

- Never hand-edit generated headers. Change `src/d2diag/signals/*.json` and regenerate.
- The sniffer must stay RX-only (passive).
