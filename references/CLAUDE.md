# references/

Working notes from reverse engineering: protocol captures, reference-tool menus, test
plans and research. The Documents tab of the dashboard serves them live.

## Files

- `test_plan.md` — **the** living car-test backlog, with decision rules.
- `protocol_state_handoff.md` — proven, candidate or open per module.
- `slabs_protocol.md`, `reference_tool_*.md` — sniffed protocol and tool menu maps.
- `*_sniff_plan.md`, `*_checklist.md`, `final_session_plan.md` — appendices to test_plan.
- `protocol_library.json` — generated by `tools/build_protocol_library.py`.
- `muki01_OBD2_K-line_Reader/` — vendored third-party reference (not indexed).

## Editing rules

- Route every car result here or to the signal store in the same commit as the code.
- Raw logs stay in the gitignored `logs/` and `captures/`. Commit derived facts only.
- New files are in English. Frontmatter is required. Rebuild INDEX.md after edits.
